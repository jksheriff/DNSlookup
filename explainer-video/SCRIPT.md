words, which is roughly 2:00 at a brisk ~150 wpm. The beat times are targets.
The final cut follows the voiceover.

**How to read some terms:** "$TRUMP" is read as "Trump". "USD1" is
"U-S-D-one". "MGX" is "M-G-X". "CZ" is "C-Z". "$2.3 billion" is "two point
three billion".

**Where the timing is tight:** Beats 6 and 7 are the densest. If your read runs
long, these words can be cut without losing any facts: "Later," (beat 7),
"Meanwhile," (beat 10) and "Then there's" (beat 5, which becomes "World Liberty
Financial is…").

## 2. Global look

**Set: the desk world.** One continuous miniature set, laid out left to right
like a timeline.

- **Wall:** mint green (`#A8E2C6`) with a pegboard panel (`#93D3B4`) that has
  hole dots, a few hooks and a small shelf.
- **Desk:** the top is pale-blue square tiles (`#8FC1EA`) with white grout. It
  has a rounded wooden front edge (`#C98B54`).
- **Floor:** light wood planks (`#E9CFA2`), seen in the wide shots.
- **Shading:** toon shading with 3 tone bands and dark ink outlines (`#1B1B1B`,
  about 3 px at 1080 wide).
- **Overlay:** a faint paper-grain texture at about 6% multiply, with a slight
  vignette.

**Camera:** a single, unbroken glide along a spline from one station to the
next. Each move eases in and out. The camera holds a gentle 2–3% drift while
the narration is speaking, and there are no hard cuts.

**Captions**

- 1–4 words, set in Archivo Black at 130–150 px, centred in the top safe zone
  (about y = 230–420).
- They pop in over 8 frames: scale goes 0.6 → 1.08 → 1.0 with a 2-frame settle.
- Each caption is triggered on its key word in the voiceover.
- Colours: black (`#141414`) by default, red (`#E63946`) for key numbers. A thin
  white stroke keeps them readable over the busy pegboard.

**Source tags:** each source appears inside the scene as a kraft-paper tag on a
string, or a rubber-stamped index card, for example "SOURCE: REUTERS". This
names the source on screen without breaking the 1–4 word caption rule.

**Objects**

- The investors are shown as faceless gray pawn figures.
- **People (revised by request):** the people the narration names appear as
  toon caricature bobbleheads with nameplates. Each one pops in on the word
  that names them.
  - **Donald Trump** appears at the hook, the coin launch, the head of the
    dinner table, the pardon and "credits rising markets".
  - **CZ** appears beside the Binance building.
  - **The Emirati royal** is a generic, unnamed figure in Gulf dress labelled
    "EMIRATI ROYAL", to match the narration.
- **Logos:** the Binance building's roof sign uses the real Binance mark, taken
  from Simple Icons (a CC0 vector; the mark itself is Binance's trademark).
  World Liberty, MGX, Reuters and the WSJ keep plain name labels, because no
  licensed logo files were available.

## 4. Fact-check notes (to settle before publishing)

1. **"Over $1.4B in token sales" (beat 5).** The coverage I found is a February
   2026 tally built on Wall Street Journal reporting. It frames about $1.4B as
   cash the venture generated for the Trump and Witkoff families over 16
   months, not as total token sales. A May 2026 Bloomberg analysis puts the
   family's WLFI token-sale take at about $1.55B. Please confirm which source
   this number comes from. The narration uses your wording.
2. **The 75% share (beat 5).** This is attributed to World Liberty's own
   disclosures. They describe the 75% as token-sale revenue *net of expenses*,
   paid to DT Marks DEFI LLC.
3. **The $500M stake (beat 7).** The source is the Wall Street Journal (Feb
   2026): an entity backed by Sheikh Tahnoon bought 49% of World Liberty.
   World Liberty also called any link to the chip decisions "100% false". That
   denial is not in the script, but it can be added.
4. **The financial disclosure (beat 8).** It covers calendar 2025 and was
   released July 2026. AP reports about $1.2B and Bloomberg about $1.4B, so
   "over $1 billion" holds. It reports revenue, not profit.
5. **The midterms (beat 10).** Confirmed: Democrats won both chambers on
   Nov 3, 2026. I could not find a source for the planned World Liberty probe,
   so please confirm it.
6. **Used as given, not independently re-checked:** the Reuters figures
   ($616M, $700M+, −97%, $2.3B, about 1M investors), and "all-time lows".
